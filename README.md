## 🌱 About Me

I'm exploring the **intersection of AI, governance, and human collaboration**—how we design systems that amplify human potential while maintaining control, transparency, and trust.

My work spans:
- **AI Governance & Management** — frameworks for responsible AI deployment
- **Agentic Systems** — intelligent agents that work *with* humans, not for them
- **Human-AI Collaboration** — interfaces and workflows that strengthen human decision-making
- **Production Automation** — reliable systems that scale with intention

## 🎯 Core Interests

### 🔐 **AI Governance**

## 💭 Philosophy

**AI is a tool for human amplification, not replacement.**

The best systems are:
- **Transparent** — you understand why the AI did what it did
- **Controllable** — humans remain in the loop and can intervene
- **Observable** — we can see what's happening in production
- **Intentional** — designed with clear purpose and guardrails

```
    Balance • Patience • Collaboration • Growth
```
